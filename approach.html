Approach

How I work

Protect the service, speed up delivery.

Good change management isn't a gate. Risk-based, automated change lets low-risk work flow and puts attention where the risk is.

Governance that stands up to audit.

Controls should be clear, measured and easy to follow. That is how you get clean audits without slowing teams down.

One source of truth.

Reliable configuration data underpins incident response, change risk, security and compliance.

Measure what matters.

Baseline availability, MTTI and MTTR, set SLOs and KPIs, then improve against them.

Use AI to augment people.

AI should make teams more efficient and give them back time for critical thinking and judgement.

Build teams that improve.

A culture of feedback, waste reduction and continual improvement, grounded in ITIL 4 and Lean.

Leadership

How I lead teams, and how I keep learning.

Servant leadership.

I lead by serving the team: setting clear direction, removing obstacles and giving people what they need to do their best work.

A culture of psychological safety.

I build a culture where people can speak up, ask questions and raise problems early, because honest feedback is how teams keep improving.

Clear direction under pressure.

In a crisis I give executives and teams clear, directive updates, so everyone knows the state of service and what happens next.

Learn from every incident.

Post-incident reviews and risk-based problem management show where issues repeat, so teams improve instead of repeating them.

Protect time to think.

Good judgement needs space. I protect time for thinking, for myself and my team, and use automation and AI to take repeatable work off people's plates.

A people strategy that enables growth.

I invest in a people strategy that supports wellbeing and a growth mindset, so people keep learning and teams keep developing.

Change enablement.

I treat change as something to enable, not impose: bringing teams with me, communicating clearly and making new ways of working easy to adopt.

Outside work

I build things to keep learning. My current project is a hands-on lab that models AI-enabled ITSM and software delivery workflows, end to end.

The lab is live, in active use and continually improved. Tools I use there: Jira for service and delivery workflows, GitHub for source control, and Cursor for AI-assisted building.

Approach

How I work

Protect the service, speed up delivery.

Good change management isn't a gate. Risk-based, automated change lets low-risk work flow and puts attention where the risk is.

Governance that stands up to audit.

Controls should be clear, measured and easy to follow. That is how you get clean audits without slowing teams down.

One source of truth.

Reliable configuration data underpins incident response, change risk, security and compliance.

Measure what matters.

Baseline availability, MTTI and MTTR, set SLOs and KPIs, then improve against them.

Use AI to augment people.

AI should make teams more efficient and give them back time for critical thinking and judgement.

Build teams that improve.

A culture of feedback, waste reduction and continual improvement, grounded in ITIL 4 and Lean.

Outside work

I build things to keep learning. My current project is a hands-on lab that models AI-enabled ITSM and software delivery workflows, end to end.

The lab is live, in active use and continually improved. Tools I use there: Jira for service and delivery workflows, GitHub for source control, and Cursor for AI-assisted building.
